Originally the name given to an extinct elephant-like mammal, Mastodon
is now best-known as a microblogging platform which is the largest part
of the "Fediverse" - a collective noun referring to inter-operable,
mostly non-corporate social media where each "instance" (a server
running that software) can federate with other instances. Mastodon
exists on thousands of servers, ranging from single-user instances to
the relative giant Mastodon.social, which was started and owned by Eugen
Rothko, the primary author of Mastodon's software.

For more, see [the Wikipedia
article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mastodon_(social_network)).
