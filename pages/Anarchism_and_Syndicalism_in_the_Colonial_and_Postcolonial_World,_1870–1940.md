**Anarchism and Syndicalism in the Colonial and Postcolonial World,
1870–1940** is a 2010 book by several historians that chronicles various
<a href="Anarchism_by_country" class="wikilink"
title="anarchist movements across the world">anarchist movements across
the world</a> from 1870 to 1940. Contributors include Benedict Anderson,
Luigi Biondi, Geoffroy de Laforcade, Arif Dirlik, Anthony Gorman, Steven
Hirsch, Dongyoun Hwang, Emmet O'Connor, Kirwin Shaffer, Aleksandr
Shubin, Edilene Toledo and Lucien van der Walt.

The full text is available is available on the theanarchistlibrary.org
here:
<https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/steven-j-hirsch-lucien-van-der-walt-anarchism-and-syndicalism-in-the-colonial-and-postcolonial>
