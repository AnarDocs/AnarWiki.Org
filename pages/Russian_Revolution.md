The **Russian Revolution** refers to two revolutions that took place in
Russia in 1917.

The first, the "February Revolution" overthrew the Russian monarchy and
led to a liberal democratic republic. The second, the "October
Revolution" saw the republic overthrown and a new state led by
<a href="Vladimir_Lenin" class="wikilink"
title="Vladimir Lenin">Vladimir Lenin</a>.
