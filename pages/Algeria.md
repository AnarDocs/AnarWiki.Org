**Algeria** is a state located in Africa.

## History

Algeria's history is of some interest to anarchists. France colonized
the country in 1830 and treated it horrendously. This eventually led to
an extremely violent and famous war of independence in 1954 that lasted
until 1962. Since then, Algeria has been independent and authoritarian.
Originally leaning towards the
<a href="Union_of_Soviet_Socialist_Republics" class="wikilink"
title="USSR">USSR</a> in the
<a href="Cold_War" class="wikilink" title="Cold War">Cold War</a>, it
has since become closer with the
<a href="United_States_of_America" class="wikilink" title="USA">USA</a>.
