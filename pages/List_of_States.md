## Active

*Note: There is significant controversy about what actually constitutes
a "state". For this list, we have chosen the 193 UN member states, with
the additions of Kosovo, Palestine, Taiwan and Vatican City. This list
is not a commentary on what states should or should not exist, but an
attempt to sketch out our current world as it does. Furthermore, we have
used the English-speaking world's common criteria of six inhabited
continents - Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania and South
America.*
